# Superstore Sales Dashboard (Excel)

A sales performance analysis of the Superstore dataset, built entirely in Excel — from raw data to a polished, presentation-ready dashboard.

## Project Overview

This project was built to demonstrate practical, hands-on data analysis skills using a real-world (if messy) sales dataset — not just coursework familiarity. The goal was to take ~9,800 rows of raw order data and turn it into clear, decision-ready business insights.

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset (Kaggle)
**Tool:** Microsoft Excel

**Techniques used:**
- Excel Tables for dynamic, structured data
- Pivot Tables (Region, Category, and Time-based breakdowns)
- VLOOKUP for exact-match lookups
- PivotCharts (bar chart for regional comparison, line chart for time trend)
- Custom-styled Dashboard sheet combining all visuals and written insights

## Key Findings

**Regional Performance**
The West leads in total sales at $725K, closely followed by the East at $679K. The Central ($501K) and South ($392K) regions contribute notably less by comparison.

**Category Performance**
Technology leads in total sales among the three categories at $836K, narrowly ahead of Furniture ($741K), with Office Supplies ($719K) close behind — all three are fairly balanced, with no single category dominating.

**Sales Trend (2014–2017)**
Sales dipped slightly at the start before rising steadily, with 2017 recording the highest sales of the period. Looking closer at the quarterly pattern, Q4 consistently records the highest sales each year — a seasonal trend likely tied to year-end corporate budgets and holiday shopping, suggesting the business should plan inventory and staffing ahead of Q4.

**Limitation**
This analysis reflects Sales only — no Profit column was available in the dataset, so these findings show what sold, not what was most profitable.
